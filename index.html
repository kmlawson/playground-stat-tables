East Asian Statistical Tables, 1929–1942

Every statistical table in English-language official yearbooks on Japan and its empire, LLM-transcribed cell by cell from page images. The figures are kept exactly as printed, including the printers' errors. Where a printed total does not add up, a transcriber's note says so, and figures that could not be read are left blank.
