Plain: printed in a table of our books · italic: used only in our English translations · dotted: added from reference data (Hangul, McCune–Reischauer, Hepburn, English Wikipedia) or the unambiguous pinyin reading of the characters; hover for the source.

Download: data/place-names.sqlite (SQLite database: places, names, every form with its system and years, and every label as printed with its book and table) · data/places.json (this table) · data/places-evidence.json (labels as printed, per place).

Warning: These names were transcribed by vision models and matched and sorted by machine. Check the tables, and the original scans linked from them, before relying on a form.
Each table can be downloaded as CSV from its page. Each book can be downloaded whole as an Excel workbook (one sheet per table, with a linked contents sheet) or as JSON; directories are available as Markdown.
