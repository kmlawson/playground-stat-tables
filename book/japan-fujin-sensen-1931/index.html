婦人職業戰線の展望 1931

Fujin shokugyō sensen no tenbō (The Front of Women's Occupations: a Survey), 東京市, 昭和6年
東京市 (Tokyo City Office), 東京, 1931

Every table is transcribed, with the figures printed on charts and the lists of survey items; the prose, essays, quoted opinions and law texts are not. In parts 1–4 the figures are kanji numerals, many printed small and heavy: they were read by Claude Opus 5.5 from tight crops and converted to Arabic figures, but in several of the occupation tables of part 2 (notably pay, allowances, bonuses, income and clothing, pp. 94–130) 三 and 五 print as identical blots at the scan's resolution and many cells could not be read — the same data are given in clear Arabic figures in the statistical appendix, read by Claude Sonnet 5.5. Where stacked strokes could be grouped more than one way (kanji) or a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 (Arabic), and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. 628 of about 53,500 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
