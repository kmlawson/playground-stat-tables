建築年鑑 1941

Kenchiku nenkan (Architectural Yearbook), 昭和16年版 (1941 edition), covering 1940
建築學會 (Architectural Institute of Japan), 東京, 1941 (昭和16年11月15日發行; 非賣品, not for sale)

Every table and list is transcribed, with the 1940 diary as a chronology; the review essays of parts II and III (only the tables and lists printed in them are given), the texts of laws and regulations and of the standards (only their tables are given), the bibliography of journal articles (IX 文獻題目, グラフ索引), the patent section XI, the society reports of XIV 1 and the advertising section are not. The fire chart (table 8) prints no figures and is not given; the figures printed on the timber chart (table 7) are, though many of its small labels could not be read. Each contractor in XVI is one table holding its balance sheet, with the firm's address, officers and accounting date in the caption; where a balance sheet does not add up (e.g. the liabilities of 池田組) the book prints it so. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5, except the sideways zoning table (一覽表 11), read by Claude Opus 5.5 from pages turned upright. In the register of completed buildings a leading ・ marks a floor area in tsubo, and ditto marks are kept as printed. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table; where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. 49 of about 19,600 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?]; unreadable characters in names are [?] or [illegible] in place.
