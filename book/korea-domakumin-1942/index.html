土幕民の生活・衞生 1942

Domakumin no seikatsu, eisei (The Life and Hygiene of the Hut Dwellers of Keijō)
京城帝國大學衞生調査部 編 (Keijō Imperial University, Hygiene Survey Department); 岩波書店, Tokyo, 1942

Every table set into the text is transcribed, with the printed contents linked to the scan; the prose, the charts and drawings without printed figures, and the reference lists are not. The tables are numbered afresh in each chapter of Parts 1–3 (the book itself prints 第三二表 twice in Part 2), and a few small unnumbered tables in the text are given with a bracketed descriptive title. Most tables print their figures in kanji numerals and were read by Claude Opus 5.5; the appendix tables of body measurements (附表) in Arabic figures were read by Claude Sonnet 5.5, and the [?] cells of those with more than four were re-read blind by Opus 5.5. Cells that could not be read with confidence are marked [?] (78 of about 15,100 filled cells, 0.5%); a plain empty cell is empty in the original. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table; where a doubtful digit could be only one of two readings (一/二/三 in kanji numerals, 3 or 8 in Arabic figures) and exactly one made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted.
