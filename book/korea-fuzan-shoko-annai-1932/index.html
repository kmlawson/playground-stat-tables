釜山の商工案内 1932

Fuzan no shōkō annai, Shōwa 7 edition (A Guide to the Commerce and Industry of Fusan)
釜山商工會議所 (Fusan Chamber of Commerce and Industry), Fusan, 1932 (昭和7年3月); compiled by 上田耕一郎 (Ueda Kōichirō)

All 31 tables and the whole trade directory (1,121 entries) are transcribed, with the three printed contents linked to the scan; the prose, the folding harbour map and the advertisement are not. Every spread was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Sonnet 5.5; the tables, set in kanji numerals, were read once by Claude Opus 5.5, and the directory and the lighter appendix lists by Claude Sonnet 5.5, from the library's full-resolution images. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted (private warehouses' capacity; the capital totals of the joint-stock, unlimited and limited partnership company lists; the joint-stock list's count of 66 firms against 65 printed). In the directory the tax amounts print their sen digits squeezed into one character width, and 63 of the 1,121 amounts could not be read with confidence (marked [?]); 7 name characters are [illegible]. In the tables two figures are unreadable and 9 name characters are [illegible]. Impossible dates (昭和三一、二 and the like) are kept as printed.
