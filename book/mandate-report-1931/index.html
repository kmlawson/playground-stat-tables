Annual Report to the League of Nations on the Administration of the South Sea Islands under Japanese Mandate for the Year 1931
Japanese Government, 1932

All statistical tables in the report and its public health appendix are transcribed; the contents, the prose, the photographs, the maps and the collection of laws and regulations are not. Tables printed sideways were read from page images turned upright. Printed totals were re-added by the readers: totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Figures that looked inconsistent with their totals were re-read by a reader who had not seen the first reading; the printed figure is kept wherever it was confirmed. The settled-account figures on printed p. 117 are faint show-through and could not be confirmed, so they are marked [?].
