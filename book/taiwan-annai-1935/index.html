台湾案内 1935

Taiwan annai (A Guide to Taiwan), by 井東憲 (Itō Ken), 昭和十年 (1935)
植民事情研究所, 東京, 1935

Only tables and lists are transcribed (35 tables, about 780 figure cells, and the list of sugar-company directors); the running text, photographs and advertisements are not. Most figures are kanji numerals, kept as printed (the site can show them in Arabic). The book is set vertically; the sugar tables printed sideways were turned upright before reading. Each page was read once by a Claude Sonnet agent; the four sugar tables, printed in squashed stacks of kanji strokes, were re-read by Claude Opus from enlarged crops. Unreadable figures are marked [?] (10 cells); 8 cells that are not legible but are the only value fitting a printed row total are shown with a blue background (inferred). Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted.
