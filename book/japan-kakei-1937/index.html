家計調査報告 1937–38

Kakei chōsa hōkoku (Report of the Household Budget Survey), 自昭和十二年九月至昭和十三年八月 (September 1937 – August 1938)
內閣統計局 (Cabinet Bureau of Statistics), 東京; issued by 東京統計協會, March 1939 (昭和14年3月)

Every table is transcribed: the small table of the survey outline, the in-text tables of the summary of results, the statistical tables 第一表–第七表 (第五表–第七表 for the whole country and each of the ten cities), the table comparing budgets before and during the China Incident, the lists and table in the survey regulations, and the classification of income and expenditure; the prose, the charts and the account-book forms are not. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from tight crops (Arabic figures). The national tables run over several spreads, each spread carrying half the columns or a block of rows: the pieces are joined into one table per printed table, with amounts (實數) and percentages (比例) as separate parts, and a check that no cell was lost or doubled. Decimal points printed as spaces or commas are written as normal figures and noted. Where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted; other doubtful digits are marked [?] even where a total would suggest a reading, and printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. No errata slip was found in this copy. 119 of about 53,500 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
