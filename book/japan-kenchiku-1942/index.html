建築年鑑 1942

Kenchiku nenkan (Architectural Yearbook), 昭和17年版 (1942 edition), covering 1941
建築學會 (Architectural Institute of Japan), 東京, 1943 (昭和18年1月30日發行; 非賣品, not for sale)

Every table and list is transcribed, with the 1941 diary as a chronology and the profiles of the main societies (XIII 1) as directory entries; the review essays of part II (only the lists printed in them are given), the texts of laws and regulations and of the standards (only their tables are given), the patent section X and the advertising section are not. Table 3 of part III (fires and fire losses by prefecture) is a bar chart that prints no figures and is not given. This edition has no register of completed buildings; the nearest thing is the index of buildings photographed in the journals (VIII). Pages 122 and 140 were transposed in printing (school rows on p. 122, city building offices on p. 140); each table is given in its proper order and says so. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 (the short list of control associations, XIII 3, by Haiku 5.5), from full-resolution crops; the sideways zoning table (一覽表 11) from pages turned upright. The small-print 目的 column of the society list (XIII 2) was read at a coarser scale and may have occasional wrong characters. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table; where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. 87 of about 12,900 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?]; unreadable characters in names are [?] in place.
