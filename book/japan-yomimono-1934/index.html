女子青年讀み物に關する調査 1934

女子青年讀み物に關する調査 附 新刊紹介 (Survey of the Reading of Young Women, with Notices of New Books), 昭和9年
大日本聯合女子青年團 (Greater Japan Federation of Young Women's Associations), 東京, 1934 (昭和9年4月1日發行; not for sale)

Every table is transcribed, with the questionnaire form, the figure lists set in the text (questions 2 and 5) and the appendix book lists; the prose is not. The book-by-prefecture grids are printed with one column per book title; they are given turned, one row per book. The figures are kanji numerals, read by Claude Opus 5.5 from tight crops and converted to Arabic figures; the questionnaire and the appendix lists were read by Claude Sonnet 5.5. Where stacked strokes could be grouped more than one way (一一 or 二, 三 or 二一) and exactly one grouping made a printed total reconcile, that grouping is used and noted (56 cells). The farming-village 'harmful books' grid was read twice independently and the readings agree on every figure. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table. 11 of about 2,070 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
