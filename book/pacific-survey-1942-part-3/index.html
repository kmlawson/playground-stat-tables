An Economic Survey of the Pacific Area, Part III: Industrialization of the Western Pacific 1942

By Kate L. Mitchell (I.P.R. Inquiry Series)
International Secretariat, Institute of Pacific Relations, New York, 1942; printed by the Haddon Craftsmen

Only the statistical tables are transcribed; the text, maps, the list of tables, the index, the publisher's list and the Institute's front matter are not. Pages with tables were found from contact sheets by Claude Sonnet 5.5, which also flagged pages printed sideways (turned upright before reading), very dense tables and tables continued over pages; a table running over several pages was read in one call with all its pages. Each page was read once and not checked by eye; unreadable figures are marked [?], and printed totals that do not add up are noted and kept as read. Tables are filed under the country chapter in which they are printed, from the book's contents.
