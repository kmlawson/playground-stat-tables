華僑ノ現勢 (The Present State of the Overseas Chinese), 昭和10年6月
外務省通商局第二課 (Second Section, Trade Bureau, Ministry of Foreign Affairs), 東京, 1935 (昭和10年6月)

Every table and list is transcribed; the prose, the regulations and the contents are not. The tables (kanji numerals) were read once by Claude Opus 5.5 from enlarged crops, the lists of names by Claude Sonnet 5.5. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted; many of the book's totals do not reconcile.
