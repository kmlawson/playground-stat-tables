家計調査報告 1940–41

Kakei chōsa hōkoku (Report of the Household Budget Survey), 自昭和十五年九月至昭和十六年八月 (September 1940 – August 1941)
內閣統計局 (Cabinet Bureau of Statistics), 東京, 1942 (昭和17年6月)

Every table is transcribed: the national and city tables of sections 二–四, the small tables in the explanation of terms and the survey rules, and the table of households allotted and selected (七); the prose, the survey regulations and the account-book forms (六) are not. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from full-resolution crops; the expenditure tables are printed in column halves on consecutive spreads and were joined row by row. Decimal points that did not print, and stray marks, are kept as printed and noted. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. 12 of about 29,000 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
