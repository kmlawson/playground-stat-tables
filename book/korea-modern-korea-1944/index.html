Modern Korea 1944

Modern Korea (Institute of Pacific Relations, International Research Series)
GRAJDANZEV Andrew J.; Institute of Pacific Relations; John Day Company, New York, 1944

All 175 tables are transcribed; the prose, maps, bibliography, index and the list of place-name equivalents are not. Every page was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Haiku, and the pages with tables were read once by Claude Haiku 5.5 from full-resolution crops of the archive's JP2 images; a pilot mission was checked figure by figure against the page (about 330 figures, no errors) before the rest were run. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Three cells are unreadable (marked [?]).
