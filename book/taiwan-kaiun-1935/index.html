臺灣の海運 1935

Taiwan no kaiun (Taiwan's Shipping)
臺灣總督府交通局遞信部 (Government-General of Taiwan, Communications Bureau), Taihoku, 1935 (昭和10年8月)

All 30 tables are transcribed, with the printed contents linked to the scan; the prose and the fold-out route maps (photographed several times in the scan) are not. Every spread was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Sonnet 5.5, and the tables, all in kanji numerals, were read once by Claude Opus 5.5. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted (among them the 1911 fare and passenger totals, the 1934 route fares, Keelung's 1928 passenger count, which appears to have dropped a digit, and several Takao cargo totals). 17 of some 1,700 figure cells are unreadable (stacked 一/二/三 strokes that no printed total settles) and marked [?].
