Annual Report of the Minister of State for Education 1904–05

Thirty-second Annual Report of the Minister of State for Education, for the Thirty-seventh Statistical Year of Meiji (1904–5), abridged
Department of Education (Mombushō), Tokyo, 1906

Each page was read once by a Claude Haiku 5.5 agent (the text sections) or a Claude Sonnet 5.5 agent (the statistical tables), from full-resolution crops, with sideways pages turned upright; pages the first Haiku reader marked hard were read again from scratch by a Sonnet 5.5 agent, whose reading replaces the first. Nothing was checked by eye. Unreadable figures are marked [?]; totals that do not add up are kept as printed. Many statistical tables are printed across two facing pages and are given as two tables (left and right halves). Only tables are transcribed; the running text is not. The scan lacks printed pp. 30–31 and 50–51. Many tiny stacked figures (foreigners, branch schools) in the dense tables and on the last property pages are too blurred to read.
