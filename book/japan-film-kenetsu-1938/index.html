活動寫眞フイルム檢閱年報 1938

Katsudō shashin firumu ken'etsu nenpō (Annual Report on Motion-Picture Film Censorship), 昭和13年 (1938)
內務省警保局 (Police Bureau, Home Ministry), 東京, compiled October 1939 (昭和十四年十月編纂)

All numbered tables (表一–表四三, of which 表三一 is printed twice), the small tables set in the prose, the appendix tables (一)–(一四) and the four statistical fold-outs (第一表–第四表) are transcribed; the prose, the classification chart of film types and the film laws (參考 映畫關係法令) are not. The chapters and appendix are printed vertically in kanji numerals and were read by Claude Opus 5.5 from tight enlarged crops and converted to Arabic figures; where stacked 一/二/三 strokes could be grouped more than one way and only one grouping made a printed total reconcile — the cell being the only doubtful one in that sum — that grouping is used and noted. The four fold-outs use Arabic figures and were read by Claude Sonnet 5.5; 第二表 was photographed as twelve overlapping tiles and was read in column strips and joined row by row (the strips agree on 1,207 of 1,210 overlapping cells); its unreadable cells were read again blind by Claude Opus 5.5. Large tables printed with the prefectures across the page are given one row per prefecture. Creases, tears and tape on the fold-outs leave some cells unreadable; printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. 32 of about 25,000 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
