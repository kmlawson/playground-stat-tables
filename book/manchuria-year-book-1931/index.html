The Manchuria Year Book 1931

Tōa Keizai Chōsakyoku (East-Asiatic Economic Investigation Bureau), Tokyo, 1931

Pages with tables were found from contact sheets by Claude Sonnet 5.5, which also flagged pages printed sideways (turned upright before reading) and tables printed across two facing pages (read as one table from both pages together). Each page was read once by Claude Haiku 5.5 from full-resolution crops, with instructions to place figures by counting the lines of each block rather than by position, because columns in this book are often printed slightly out of line with their row labels; it was not checked by eye. Unreadable figures are marked, printed totals that do not add up are noted and kept as read, and tables continued over pages were joined where the column headings match. Only tables and columnar lists are transcribed; the running text, photographs, maps, the contents and the index are not. The chronology of 1925–1930 (Appendix) is on the Chronologies page, each page read once by Claude Haiku 5.5; dates carried over from a year or month heading are written out in full. The lists of banks and of social welfare institutions are on the Directories page.
