臺灣社會敎育概要 1935

Taiwan shakai kyōiku gaiyō (Outline of Social Education in Taiwan), 昭和十年十月 (October 1935)
臺灣總督府 (Government-General of Taiwan), 臺北, 1935 (昭和10年10月)

Every table and columnar list set into the text is transcribed (58), with the printed contents; the prose, the rescripts, the numbered policy points, the charts (which print only their scales), the photo plates and the rules of the moral-education federations (including the lists of officers in their articles) are not. Every page of the body was opened by a reader: Claude Opus 5.5 for the pages with kanji-numeral tables, Claude Sonnet 5.5 for the pages of prose, each read once from tight crops; there was no second reading. The kanji numerals are written as Arabic figures. Printed totals were re-added: many do not add up, often by a round 10, 100 or 1,000, and are kept as printed and noted. Where the only doubt was how stacked 一/二/三 strokes group and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. The errata slip on the front endpaper (corrected figures for the village moral-education associations in the overview table on p. 6, whose printed row is overwritten by hand on this copy) is attached as a note. Several tables are printed in very small type that is blurred on the scan — the overview table on p. 6, the two national-language institute tables on pp. 51–52 and the itemised local expenditure on pp. 132–134 — and most of their figures could not be read: 550 of about 4,200 filled cells are marked [?], nearly all of them in those six tables.
