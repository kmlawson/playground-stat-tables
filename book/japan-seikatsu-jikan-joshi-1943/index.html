國民生活時間調査 俸給生活者・工場勞務者 女子家族編 1943

Kokumin seikatsu jikan chōsa: ippan chōsa hōkoku, hōkyū seikatsusha・kōjō rōmusha joshi kazoku hen (National Time-Use Survey, General Survey Report: Female Family Members of Salaried Employees and Factory Workers), 昭和18年 (通巻23)
日本放送協會 (Japan Broadcasting Corporation, NHK), 東京, 1943

Every table is transcribed: the 10 national and 20 regional time-of-day tables, the four time-budget tables, the 20 regional listening-suitability tables and the small tables and district lists in the outline. The 10 listening-suitability charts (時刻別聽取適否參考圖) print no figures and are not reproduced; the prose is not transcribed. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from full-resolution crops, with no checking by eye. Where a 3 could be an 8 and only one reading makes the row add to 100, that reading is used and noted. Rows that do not add to 100 as printed (rounding, and some dropped or wrong digits in the type) are kept as printed and noted. 171 of about 119,000 cells could not be read and are marked [?].
