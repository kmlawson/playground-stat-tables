建築年鑑 1940

Kenchiku nenkan (Architectural Yearbook), 昭和15年版 (1940 edition), covering 1939
建築學會 (Architectural Institute of Japan), 東京, 1940 (昭和15年7月31日發行); sold by 丸善

Every table and list is transcribed, with the 1939 diary as a chronology; the review essays of part II, the texts of laws and regulations (only the small tables printed in them are given), the articles of the steel and timber standards (only their tables are given), the bibliography of journal articles (VIII 文獻題目, グラフ索引), the patent section X (prose with drawings), the society reports of XII 1 and the advertising section are not. Charts that print no figures (tables 2 (2), 5 (5)–(6), 7 (2), 10 and 11 of part III) are not given; the figures printed on the timber chart (table 9) are. The tables were read by Claude Sonnet 5.5, except the sideways zoning table (關係一覽表 11), read by Claude Opus 5.5 from pages turned upright. On the badly printed budget table 4 of part III, 34 doubtful cells were read a second time, blind, by Opus; where both readers saw a digit that does not make the printed total add up, the digit is kept as printed. In the register of completed buildings a leading ・ marks a floor area in tsubo, and ditto marks are kept as printed. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table; where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. 14 of about 21,500 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
