日本帝國委任統治地域行政年報 1929

Nihon teikoku inin tōchi chiiki gyōsei nenpō (Annual Report on the Administration of the Territory under Japanese Mandate), 千九百二十九年度 (for 1929)
外務省 (Ministry of Foreign Affairs), 東京

All tables in the report and its appendix are transcribed; the prose, the organisation chart and the contents are not. The tables are printed vertically in kanji numerals; they are written here horizontally in Arabic figures, with 、 kept as a comma (also where it serves as a decimal mark, as in the 方里 areas). Printed totals were re-added by the readers: totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Where only the grouping of stacked 一/二/三 strokes was in doubt and exactly one grouping made a printed total reconcile, that grouping was used; such cells were re-read by a reader who had not seen the first reading, and cells not confirmed are marked [?]. The crime tables, the trade tables and the table of hospital patients by disease were transcribed a second time independently; where the two readings differed a third blind reading decided, and cells on which no two readings agreed are marked [?].
