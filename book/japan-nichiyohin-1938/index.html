東京市日用品買入先調査書 1938

Tōkyō-shi nichiyōhin kaiiresaki chōsasho (Survey of Where Tokyo Households Buy Daily Necessities), 昭和十三年三月 (March 1938)
東京市役所, 東京, 1938 (昭和十三年三月二十日發行)

All tables are transcribed (69 tables, about 15,000 figure cells), with the contents; the preface, 凡例 text, the blank questionnaire and the running text are not. Every appendix table is printed as a count table and a percentage table (同上百分比表), given here as separate tables; the three spreads of appendix tables 1, 2 and 4 are the city total and the two market districts, given as separate tables or parts. The book is hand-lettered on stencil: percentages are written with a comma-like decimal mark, given here as a point. Each spread was read once by a Claude Sonnet agent from tight crops, with no second reading. Unreadable figures are marked [?] (58 cells); a plain empty cell is empty in the original. Printed totals and percentages that do not add up are kept as printed and noted; two cells printed without their decimal mark and placed by the row total of 100.00 are shown with a blue background (inferred).
