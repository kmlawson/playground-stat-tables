家計調査報告 1931–32

Kakei chōsa hōkoku (Report of the Household Budget Survey), 自昭和六年九月至昭和七年八月 (September 1931 – August 1932)
內閣統計局 (Cabinet Bureau of Statistics), 東京; issued by 東京統計協會, 15 September 1933 (昭和8年9月15日)

Every table is transcribed: the table of households allotted in the survey outline, the in-text tables of the summary of results, the statistical tables 第一表–第七表, the tables in the survey regulations, and the classification of income and expenditure; the prose, the charts and the account-book forms are not. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from tight crops (Arabic figures). The statistical tables run over several spreads, each spread carrying half the columns or a block of rows: the pieces are joined into one table per printed table, with amounts (實數) and percentages (比例) as separate parts, and a check that no cell was lost or doubled. Where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted; other doubtful digits are marked [?], and printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Damaged and clipped digits are frequent in the statistical tables of this copy. No errata slip was found. 167 of about 31,100 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
