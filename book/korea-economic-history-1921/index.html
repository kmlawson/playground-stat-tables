Economic History of Chosen 1921

Economic History of Chosen, compiled by the Bank of Chosen
HOSHINO Tokuji (comp.); Bank of Chosen (Chōsen Ginkō), Seoul, 1921

All 113 tables are transcribed; the prose, photo plates, the Bank of Chosen Act and By-Laws (Appendices I–II) and the index are not. Every page was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Haiku, and the pages with tables were read once by Claude Haiku 5.5 from the archive's JP2 images; a pilot mission was checked figure by figure against the page before the rest were run, and the dense fold-out balance sheet (p. 236) was read a second time independently by Claude Sonnet 5.5. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. About 70 of some 7,200 figure cells are unreadable on this scan (broken or faint type) and are marked [?].
