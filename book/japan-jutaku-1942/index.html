住宅問題統計調査資料 1942

Jūtaku mondai tōkei chōsa shiryō (Statistical Materials on the Housing Problem)
住宅營團研究部 編 (Housing Corporation, Research Department), 住宅營團, Tokyo, 昭和17年7月 (July 1942); printed by 一成社

Every table is transcribed, with the printed contents; the foreword is not. The scan is a black-and-white microfilm copy with broken strokes and specks; the vertical tables with kanji numerals (pp. 4, 12–15, 58–63) were read by Opus, the rest by Sonnet. Unreadable cells are marked [?] (83 of about 12,400 figures, 0.7%); a plain empty cell is empty in the original. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted; where the only doubt was a 3 or 8 (or how a stack of 一/二/三 strokes groups) and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted.
