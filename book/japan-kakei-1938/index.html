家計調査報告 1938–39

Kakei chōsa hōkoku (Report of the Household Budget Survey), 自昭和十三年九月至昭和十四年八月 (September 1938 – August 1939)
內閣統計局 (Cabinet Bureau of Statistics), 東京; issued by 東京統計協會, 1940 (昭和15年5月)

Every table is transcribed: the small tables of the survey outline, the in-text tables of the summary of results, the statistical tables 第一表–第七表 (第五表–第七表 for the whole country and each of the ten cities), the tables in the survey regulations, and the classification of income and expenditure; the prose and the account-book forms are not. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from tight crops (Arabic figures); one city table left between two readers (Kanazawa, 第六表) was read by a further mission. The national tables run over several spreads, each spread carrying half the columns for a block of rows: the pieces are joined into one table per printed table, with amounts (實數) and percentages (比例) as separate parts, and a check that no cell was lost or doubled. Decimal points printed as spaces, hyphens, commas, colons or raised dots are written as normal figures and noted. Where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted; printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. No errata slip was found in this copy. Cells the first reader could not make out in tables with more than four such cells were read again blind by Claude Opus 5.5 (3 of 12 filled; readings the second reader marked doubtful were not used). 39 of about 52,700 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
