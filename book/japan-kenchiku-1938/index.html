建築年鑑 1938

Kenchiku nenkan (Architectural Yearbook), 昭和13年版 (1938 edition), covering 1937
建築學會 (Architectural Institute of Japan), 東京, 1938 (昭和13年7月9日發行); sold by 丸善

Every table and list is transcribed, with the 1937 diary as a chronology; the review essays of part II, the texts of laws and regulations (only the small tables printed in them are given), the bibliography of journal articles (VII 文獻題目, グラフ索引), the society reports of XI 1, the photo plates and the advertising section are not. Charts that print no figures (tables 3 (2), 6 (5)–(6), 8 (2), 14 and 18) are not given; the figures printed on the pie charts of the map of architects are. The scan lacks the leaf with pp. 76–77 (part II), and pp. 274–291 are bound out of order (the scan links follow the binding). In the register of completed buildings a leading ・ marks a floor area in tsubo, and ditto marks are kept as printed. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table; where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. 43 of about 28,000 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
