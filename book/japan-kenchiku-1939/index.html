建築年鑑 1939

Kenchiku nenkan (Architectural Yearbook), 昭和14年版 (1939 edition), covering 1938
建築學會 (Architectural Institute of Japan), 東京, 1939 (昭和14年7月1日發行); sold by 丸善

Every table and list is transcribed, with the 1938 diary as a chronology; the review essays of parts II and III (only the tables and lists printed in them are given), the texts of laws and regulations (only the small tables printed in them are given), the bibliography of journal articles (VII 文獻題目, グラフ索引), the patent lists of IX, the society reports of XI 1 and the advertising section are not. Charts that print no figures (tables 2 (2), 5 (5)–(6), 7 (2), 13 and 15 of part III) are not given; the figures printed on the timber chart (table 9) and the Institute's membership chart are. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5, except the sideways zoning table (關係一覽表 8), read by Claude Opus 5.5 from pages turned upright. Columns lost in the binding gutter (four columns of table 2 (1), one of 5 (4)) are marked [?]. In the register of completed buildings a leading ・ marks a floor area in tsubo, and ditto marks are kept as printed. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table; where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. 203 of about 25,200 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?]; unreadable characters in names are [?] or [illegible] in place.
