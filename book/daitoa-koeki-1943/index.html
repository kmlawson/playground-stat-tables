大東亞交易基本統計表 1943

Daitōa kōeki kihon tōkeihyō (Basic Trade Statistics of Greater East Asia)
日本貿易振興協會 日本貿易研究所東京調査部 編 (Japan Trade Research Institute, Tokyo Research Department), 栗田書店, Tokyo, 昭和18年5月 (May 1943)

Every table in the four parts and the two appendices is transcribed, with the printed contents; the prefaces and explanatory notes are given as footnotes. The commodity tables of Parts 2 and 3 run over several spreads (row blocks and column groups); each spread is one part of its table, as printed. Read by Sonnet sub-agents from the page images. Unreadable cells are marked [?] (153 of about 57,000 figures, 0.3%); a plain empty cell is empty in the original. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted; decimal points too faint to see are either supplied (noted) or the figures kept as printed with a space.
