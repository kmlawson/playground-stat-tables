京都市社會課 調査報告 第四十一號 (Survey of Korean-Born Residents of Kyoto City, Kyoto City Social Section Report No. 41), 昭和12年1月
京都市社會課 (Kyoto City Social Section), 京都, 1937 (昭和12年1月30日發行; not for sale)

Every table is transcribed, including the small tables set in the text, with the list of the Social Section's reports at the back; the prose, the preface and the blank survey forms are not. Almost all tables are unnumbered and are given with the title printed above them, or a description in [brackets]. The figures are kanji numerals squeezed two or three to a square; they were read by Claude Opus 5.5 from tight enlarged crops and converted to Arabic figures (the contents and the report list by Claude Sonnet 5.5). Where stacked strokes could be grouped more than one way (一一 or 二, 三 or 二一 or 一二) and exactly one grouping made a printed total reconcile, that grouping is used and noted. The school-district table (pp. 44–48), in very small type, left 314 cells unread at the first reading; these and the other unread cells were read again blind (the reader saw only the table, row and column) by Claude Opus 5.5, and a reading was used only where that reader was certain (242 cells, each noted). Printed totals and percentages that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table. 100 of about 15,300 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?], most of them in the school-district table.
