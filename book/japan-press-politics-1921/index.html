The Press and Politics in Japan 1921

A Study of the Relation between the Newspaper and the Political Development of Modern Japan
KAWABÉ Kisaburō; University of Chicago Press, Chicago, 1921

All 18 tables are transcribed; the rest of the book (narrative, bibliography and index) is prose and is not. Every page was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Haiku, and the 13 pages with tables were read once by Claude Haiku from full-resolution crops. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted (one, the railways table, was checked against the page by eye: the book's own sums are off). No cell is unreadable.
