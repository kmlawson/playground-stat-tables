大商店就業時間休日調査 1931

Daishōten ni okeru shūgyō jikan kyūjitsu tō ni kansuru chōsa gaiyō (Outline of a Survey of Working Hours, Holidays etc. in Large Shops), 昭和6年11月
社會局 (Social Bureau, Home Ministry), 東京, 1932

Every table in the report is transcribed, with two lists of special duties set into the text; the running prose and the diagrams of shift patterns are not. The figures are printed vertically in kanji numerals and are given here in Arabic figures; durations printed as hours and minutes are written H:MM. Each spread was read once by Claude Opus 5.5 from full-resolution crops, with no checking by eye; two cells whose 一/二/三 strokes could be grouped more than one way were settled by the printed total and are noted. Printed totals and percentages that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. No cell was left unread.
