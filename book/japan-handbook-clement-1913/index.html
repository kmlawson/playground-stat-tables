A Handbook of Modern Japan (revised edition)
CLEMENT Ernest W.; A. C. McClurg & Co., Chicago, 1913

Every page was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Haiku, and the pages with tables and chronologies were read once by Claude Haiku 5.5 from the archive's JP2 images (two sideways tables turned upright first). Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. No cell is unreadable.
