Shanghai Municipal Council Report 1881

Report for the Year ending 31st December 1881 and Budget for the Year 1882
Shanghai Municipal Council, Shanghai, 1882

All tables and lists are transcribed, including small tables set in the text, the accounts and the budget (each sub-head of the financial statement and budget is a separate table); the prose and letters are not. The Fire Record (a dated list of fires) is given as a chronology. The volume prints no table of contents (only an alphabetical index), so the book's contents list here is made of bracketed section headings in the order of the volume. The scan is of lower resolution than later volumes. Table pages were found from contact sheets by Claude Sonnet 5.5; light pages were read once by Claude Haiku 5.5, dense and wide pages by Claude Sonnet 5.5. Not checked by eye. Printed totals were re-added by the readers: totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Figures that could not be read are marked [?] (2 cells). The budget has its own page numbers, as printed. The scan lacks budget pages 13–14 and 19–20, so the Public Works estimate, part of the income and expenditure and the first part of the Summary of Expenditure are incomplete; those tables say so.
