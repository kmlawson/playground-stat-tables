中華民國統計提要 1947

Statistical Abstract of the Republic of China, 1947 (民國三十六年)
國民政府主計處統計局 (Directorate of Statistics, National Government), Nanking, 1947

All 80 tables are transcribed, with their Chinese and English titles, headings and row labels as printed. Every page was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Sonnet; the 98 pages printed sideways were turned upright before reading. Each table was read once, by Claude Haiku (simple one-page tables) or Claude Sonnet (multi-page tables and tables printed across two facing pages, which were read as one). The scan is a rough 300 dpi black-and-white image with broken and doubled type in places: the 266 cells the first readers could not read were re-read blind (without seeing the rest of the table) by Claude Opus, which settled 122 of them; the remaining 144 (0.8% of 18,319 figure cells) are marked [?]. Printed totals were re-added and a total that does not reconcile is kept as printed with a note. A few figures are printed half a line below their row labels; these were matched to their rows by order, as noted in the tables.
