朝鮮の巫覡 1932

Chōsen no fugeki (The Shamans of Korea), 朝鮮總督府 調査資料
朝鮮總督府 (Government-General of Chōsen), Keijō, 1932 (昭和7年3月31日); by 村山智順 (Murayama Chijun)

All 18 tables are transcribed, with the printed contents linked to the scan; the prose (including the long region-by-region descriptions in chapters 4–7), the appendix of prayer texts and the photographs are not. Every spread was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Sonnet 5.5; the tables in kanji numerals were read once by Claude Opus 5.5, and the list of local names and the four continuation parts of the survey-answer list by Claude Sonnet 5.5. The survey-answer list prints no province headings: its 道 column is filled from the county as listed in the book's own table of local names. Its bracketed place names are taken as closing each group of answers; on p. 54 they may instead open them (noted on the table). One printed total does not add up (成巫動機別統計表, 世襲 column: 136 printed, parts 137); one character is [illegible]. The 18 corrections of the tipped-in errata slip all concern the prose.
