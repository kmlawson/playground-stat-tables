東京市内職調査 1935

Tōkyō-shi naishoku chōsa (Survey of Home Work in Tokyo City), 昭和十年度 (1935 survey, published March 1936)
東京市社會局 (Social Bureau, Tokyo City), 東京, 1936

Every table is transcribed: the tables in the outline, the four statistical tables of Part 2 and the figures printed under the four bar charts (the bars themselves are not read); the prose and the blank survey form are not. The errata slip's corrections to Table 3 are given in that table's notes. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from full-resolution crops, with no checking by eye; the long tables were read spread by spread and joined. Where a 3 could be an 8 and only one reading makes a printed total reconcile — the cell being the only doubtful one in that sum — that reading is used and noted. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. 27 of about 52,000 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
