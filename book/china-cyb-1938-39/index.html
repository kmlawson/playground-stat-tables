The Chinese Year Book 1938-39

1938-39 Issue (Fourth Year of Publication)
Council of International Affairs, Chungking; printed and distributed by the Commercial Press, 1939

Only tables and columnar lists are transcribed; prose, advertisements, maps, graphs and the index are not. Pages with tables were found from contact sheets by Claude Sonnet 5.5, which also flagged pages printed sideways (turned upright before reading), very dense tables and tables continued over pages. A table running over several pages was read in one call with all its pages. Each page was read once and not checked by eye; unreadable figures are marked [?], and printed totals that do not add up are noted and kept as read. The contents were read once by Claude Haiku 5.5.
