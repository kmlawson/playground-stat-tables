學生生徒兒童身長・體重・胸圍平均累年比較 (明治33～昭和9年度) / 公私立小學校・中學校・高等女學校生徒兒童身體檢査統計 (昭和8・9年度) (Height, Weight and Chest Girth of Pupils and Students, 1900–1934, and School Physical-Examination Statistics for 1933 and 1934)
文部大臣官房體育課 (Ministry of Education, Minister's Secretariat, Physical Education Section), 東京, 1937 (昭和12年3月30日發行)

Every table is transcribed; the book has no printed contents, so the contents shown here were made from the table headings (part titles in brackets). Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from tight crops. Many decimal points did not print (a gap is left in the type); since every figure in a table has the same number of decimals, these are given with the point and listed in each table's notes. No 大正十年 (1921) row is printed in the year-by-year tables. Rows of percentages that do not add up to 100, and figures that look out of line with their neighbours, are kept as printed and noted. Where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. 71 of about 25,200 figures could not be read and are marked [?].
