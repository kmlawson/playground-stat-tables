クーポン旅館一覧 1936

Kūpon ryokan ichiran (List of Coupon Ryokan), 昭和十一年十一月 (November 1936)
社團法人ジャパン・ツーリスト・ビューロー（日本旅行協會）, 東京 (昭和十一年)

All ryokan listings (50 tables, one per prefecture or territory as printed), the Bureau's offices, the transport guide (one row per place) and the colophon are transcribed; the prose on buying and using coupons and the reply forms are not. The column headings printed on p. 6 are used for every listing, as the pamphlet does not repeat them. The telephone numbers are printed in very small stacked kanji numerals; those that could not be read with confidence are marked [?]. Read by Claude Opus agents, one spread per mission; not checked by a second reader.
