臺灣社會敎育概要 1937

Taiwan shakai kyōiku gaiyō (Outline of Social Education in Taiwan), 昭和十二年二月 (February 1937)
臺灣總督府 (Government-General of Taiwan), 臺北, 1937 (昭和12年2月)

Every table and columnar list set into the text is transcribed (22), with the printed contents; the prose, the rescripts, the numbered policy points, the organisation chart, the photo plates and the rules of the federations in the appendix (including the lists of officers in their articles) are not. Every page of the body was opened by a reader: Claude Opus 5.5 for the pages with kanji-numeral tables, Claude Sonnet 5.5 for the pages of prose, each read once from tight crops; there was no second reading. The kanji numerals are written as Arabic figures. Printed totals were re-added: totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Where the only doubt was how stacked 一/二/三 strokes group and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. 1 of about 1,200 filled cells could not be read and is marked [?].
