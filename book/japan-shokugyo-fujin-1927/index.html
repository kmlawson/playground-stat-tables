職業婦人調査 1927

東京大阪兩市に於ける職業婦人調査 タイピスト・事務員・交換手・店員 (Survey of Working Women in Tokyo and Osaka: Typists, Office Clerks, Telephone Operators, Shop Assistants) and 職業婦人調査 看護婦・産婆 (Nurses and Midwives), 昭和2年3月; two reports bound together
中央職業紹介事務局 (Central Employment Exchange Bureau), 東京, 1927 (昭和2年3月; the nurses and midwives volume 昭和2年3月28日發行)

Every table is transcribed, with the figures printed on the chart plates and the lists of training schools and institutions; the prose, regulations, blank forms and examination questions are not. The scan shows several chart plates and the fold-out tables twice (a dark or partial copy, then a full one); the full copies were read. The figures are kanji numerals, many squeezed two or three to a square; they were read by Claude Opus 5.5 from tight crops and converted to Arabic figures (contents, regulation sections and lists by Claude Sonnet 5.5). Where stacked strokes could be grouped more than one way and exactly one grouping made a printed total reconcile, that grouping is used and noted. The midwives' examination fold-out is printed in very small type: its unreadable cells were read a second time, blind, by Claude Opus 5.5, and only confident readings were filled in. Printed totals and percentages that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Table numbers restart in each occupation part. 89 of about 22,000 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
