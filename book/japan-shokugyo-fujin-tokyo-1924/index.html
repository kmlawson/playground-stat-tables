職業婦人に關する調査 1924

職業婦人に關する調査 (Survey of Working Women), 大正13年12月
東京市社會局 (Tokyo City Social Bureau), 東京, 1924 (大正13年12月)

Every table is transcribed, with the lists of companies, the reading-matter lists, and the directories of schools and women's organisations; the prose, the numbered free-text answers and the eleven chart plates (which print only their axis scales) are not. Tables were read once by Claude Opus agents from enlarged crops (the figures are stacked kanji numerals); the directories of schools and organisations by Claude Sonnet, with the school list re-read by Opus after the first reading mismatched its cells. The list of workplaces (pp. 179–202) is printed in very small squeezed type: about 150 of its figures could not be read and are marked [?]. In all, about 250 of some 12,600 figure cells are marked [?], and one American table (p. 224) is mostly unreadable on this scan. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. The book's errata slip (正誤表) is noted on the tables it concerns; the figures stay as printed. The tables on reading matter and organisations are printed as 第三十表 and 第三十一表, although the list of tables calls them 第三十三表 and 第三十四表.
