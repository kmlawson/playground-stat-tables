朝鮮總督府統計年報 1922 第3編其ノ1

Chōsen Sōtokufu tōkei nenpō (Statistical Annual of the Government-General of Korea), 大正十一年 (Taishō 11), Part 3, No. 1: 商業、工業・物價及勞銀
朝鮮總督府, 京城, 1924 (大正十三年)

All 18 numbered tables are transcribed (21 tables, about 16,477 figure cells), with the contents; the 凡例, the contents pages and the colophon are not. One frame is a re-shot duplicate and was skipped; the long price tables are faint in places. Tables printed across both pages of a spread are joined into one; long tables were read in sections by different readers and joined. Each spread was read once by a Claude Sonnet agent from tight crops, with no second reading. 284 cells could not be read and are marked [?]. Printed totals were re-added; those that do not add up are kept as printed and noted, and where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. Where the original prints a question mark (unknown figures for early years) it is kept as ?.
