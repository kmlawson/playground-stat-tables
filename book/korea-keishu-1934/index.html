生活狀態調査 慶州郡 1934

生活狀態調査(其七) 慶州郡 (Survey of Living Conditions, no. 7: Kyŏngju County), 朝鮮總督府 調査資料 第四十輯
朝鮮總督府 (Government-General of Korea), 京城, 1934 (昭和9年2月28日發行; preface 昭和8年12月)

Every table is transcribed, including the small tables and lists set in the text (lists of names and things without figures are given as tables or directory entries); the prose, laws and association rules, song texts, documents reproduced in facsimile, maps, house plans and the photograph plates are not. The figures are kanji numerals, read by Claude Opus 5.5 from tight enlarged crops and converted to Arabic figures (the contents and three long lists by Claude Sonnet 5.5). Where stacked strokes could be grouped more than one way (一一 or 二, 三 or 二一 or 一二) and exactly one grouping made a printed total reconcile — the cell being the only doubtful one in that sum — that grouping is used and noted. The household basic-survey table (pp. 526–536) was read twice by different missions; where the two readings differ the cell is marked [?] and both readings are noted. Ratio tables that print 、 as the decimal mark are given with a decimal point. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table; the surname table (pp. 237–244) has many. 205 of about 21,800 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
