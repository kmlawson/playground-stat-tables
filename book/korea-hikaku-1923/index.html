朝鮮・內地・臺灣比較統計要覽 1923

Chōsen, Naichi, Taiwan hikaku tōkei yōran (Comparative Statistics of Korea, Japan Proper and Taiwan), figures mostly for 大正十二年 (1923)
朝鮮總督府, 京城, c. 1924–25

All 31 tables are transcribed (about 1,340 figure cells), with the contents; the explanatory notes (凡例) are partly covered by a library slip and stamp. The scan is a small black-and-white copy: a crease and library stamps hide some cells, and several tables lose their last column at the edge of the scan. Each page was read by a Claude Sonnet agent from enlarged crops; the 123 cells it could not read were then re-read by Claude Opus agents from tighter crops (2026-11-24): 27 were read, 27 that a printed total fixes exactly (one unknown, every other figure read) are filled in and shown with a blue background (inferred), and 69 remain [?] (mostly cells in the crease of p. 3 and columns cut off at the edge). Printed totals were re-added and those that do not add up are noted. The book has no date of publication in this copy; its figures are for 1923.
