朝鮮の犯罪と環境 1928

Chōsen no hanzai to kankyō (Crime and Its Setting in Korea), 朝鮮總督府 調査資料 第二十三輯
朝鮮總督府 (Government-General of Chōsen), Keijō, 1928 (序 昭和3年3月); by 善生永助 (Zenshō Eisuke)

All 78 tables are transcribed, with the printed contents linked to the scan; the prose is not. Every spread was surveyed on contact sheets by Claude Sonnet 5.5, and the tables, all in kanji numerals, were read once by Claude Opus 5.5 in 42 missions. The figures are small stacked kanji strokes (一/二/三) that are often hard to group at the scan's resolution: 716 of 25,609 figure cells (2.8%) are marked [?], most of them in the police-station tables of 第三章 (慶尙北道 183, 全羅南道 108). A printed total settled a figure only where the strokes were certain and only their grouping was in doubt; cells that readers settled in any other way were set back to [?] with a note. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Tables printed with the stations or counties across the top are given with them as rows.
