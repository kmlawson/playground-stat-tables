The Financial and Economic Annual of Japan 1901

Financial Annual of Japan, No. I
Department of Finance, Tokyo (Government Press), 1901

Only tables are transcribed; the running text and notes are not. Table pages were found from contact sheets; no table is printed sideways. Most tables run across two facing pages (years and first columns on the left, further columns on the right), so each two-page spread was read as one image set and such tables joined into one. Each spread or page was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 (large tables) or Claude Haiku 5.5 (simpler pages) through the llm CLI, with no second reading and no check by eye. Unreadable figures are marked [?]. The scan repeats printed pp. 28–29 (read once) and lacks pp. 38–39 (reserve against convertible notes and the start of the clearing-house table).
