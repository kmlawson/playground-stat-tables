Annual Report of the Minister of State for Education 1891

Nineteenth Annual Report of the Minister of State for Education, for the Twenty-fourth Year of Meiji (1891)
Department of Education (Mombushō), Tokyo, 1893

Each page was read once by a Claude Haiku 5.5 agent (the text sections and the first half of the statistics) or a Claude Sonnet 5.5 agent (the dense statistical tables), from full-resolution crops, with sideways pages turned upright; pages the first Haiku reader marked hard were read again from scratch by a Sonnet 5.5 agent, whose reading replaces the first. Nothing was checked by eye. Unreadable figures are marked [?]; totals that do not add up are kept as printed. Most statistical tables are printed across two facing pages and are given here as two tables (left and right halves); right halves carry the row labels of the facing page. Only tables are transcribed; the running text is not. The scan repeats printed pp. 144–145 (read once).
