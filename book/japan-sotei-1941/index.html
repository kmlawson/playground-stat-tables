壯丁教育調査概況 昭和16年度 (Survey of the Education of Conscripts, 1941)
文部省國民教育局 (Ministry of Education, National Education Bureau), 東京, 1943 (凡例 dated 昭和18年3月; marked 秘, confidential)

Every table is transcribed, with the small tables set in the text; the prose, the survey forms and the test papers (pp. 6–7, 55–60) are not. The 正誤表 (errata slip) bound at the back is given as a table, and each correction that falls in a table is noted on that table (the cell stays as printed). Charts that print no figures are not given. The year-by-year prefectural table (第6表, eight spreads) was read spread by spread and joined. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from tight crops. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table; where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. 34 of about 27,600 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
