職業婦人調査 女給 1926

東京大阪兩市に於ける職業婦人調査 女給 (Survey of Working Women in Tokyo and Osaka: Café Waitresses), 大正15年3月
中央職業紹介事務局 (Central Employment Exchange Bureau), 東京, 1926 (大正15年3月31日發行)

Every table is transcribed, with the questionnaire form, the return figures by police district, the lists of answers with counts and the figures printed on the chart plates; the prose, the conclusion and the chapter on cafés, bars and restaurants (no tables) are not. The scan repeats the plate of Figure 5 and shows the fold-out of pp. 78–79 twice (with the flap open and turned back). The figures are kanji numerals, many squeezed two or three to a square; they were read by Claude Opus 5.5 from tight crops and converted to Arabic figures (the questionnaire, the plates and the contents by Claude Sonnet 5.5). Where stacked strokes could be grouped more than one way (一一 or 二, 三 or 二一 or 一二) and exactly one grouping made a printed total reconcile, that grouping is used and noted; readings that went further than that were turned back to [?]. Printed totals and percentages that do not add up are kept as printed and noted; several district tables disagree slightly with the summary tables. Table numbers are as printed: there is no Table 39, and both tip tables by police district are numbered 40. 23 of about 18,000 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
