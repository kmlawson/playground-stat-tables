家計調査報告 1933–34

Kakei chōsa hōkoku (Report of the Household Budget Survey), 自昭和八年九月至昭和九年八月 (September 1933 – August 1934)
內閣統計局 (Cabinet Bureau of Statistics), 東京; issued by 東京統計協會, 29 March 1935 (昭和10年3月29日)

Every table is transcribed: the table of households allotted in the survey outline, the in-text tables of the summary of results, the statistical tables 第一表–第七表, the tables in the survey regulations, and the classification of income and expenditure; the prose, the charts and the account-book forms are not. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from tight crops (Arabic figures). The statistical tables run over several spreads, each spread carrying half the columns or a block of rows: the pieces are joined into one table per printed table, with amounts (實數) and percentages (比例) as separate parts, and a check that no cell was lost or doubled. Where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted; other doubtful digits are marked [?], and printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. The volume's errata slip (正誤表, 6 corrections) is attached as a note to each table it concerns; the figures stay as printed. 15 of about 29,900 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
