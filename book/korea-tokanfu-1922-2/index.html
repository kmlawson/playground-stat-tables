朝鮮總督府統計年報 1922 第2編

Chōsen Sōtokufu tōkei nenpō (Statistical Annual of the Government-General of Korea), 大正十一年 (Taishō 11), Part 2: 農業・林業附狩獵・水產業・鑛業
朝鮮總督府, 京城, 1924 (大正十三年)

All 65 numbered tables are transcribed (70 tables, about 20,888 figure cells), with the contents; the 凡例, the contents pages and the colophon are not. Divider leaves carry small summary boxes, transcribed as unnumbered tables. Tables printed across both pages of a spread are joined into one; long tables were read in sections by different readers and joined. Each spread was read once by a Claude Sonnet agent from tight crops, with no second reading. 125 cells could not be read and are marked [?]. Printed totals were re-added; those that do not add up are kept as printed and noted, and where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. Where the original prints a question mark (unknown figures for early years) it is kept as ?.
