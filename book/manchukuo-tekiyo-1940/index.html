滿洲帝國統計摘要 1940

Manshū teikoku tōkei tekiyō (Statistical Abstract of Manchoukuo), 康德六年版 (1939 edition)
國務院總務廳統計處 (General Affairs Board, Statistics Office), 新京, August 1940

Every table in the book is transcribed, with the printed contents linked to the scan; the 民營自動車 table is printed with headings only (its figures were withheld under the 國家總動員 secrecy rules) and is given as printed. Many tables are printed sideways and were rotated upright before reading; tables that run across both pages of a spread are one table, the right-page figures matched to the left-page row labels by order, and the long 市縣旗別人口 table (pp. 10–29) was read in pieces and joined. Read by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from crops of each page; cells that could not be read with confidence are marked [?] (86 of about 26,500 filled cells, 0.3%), and the [?] cells of the four tables with more than four were re-read blind by Opus 5.5. A plain empty cell is empty in the original. Printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted in each table (there are many misprints); where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. Page 218 is scanned twice (frames 120 and 121; the clearer frame 121 was read).
