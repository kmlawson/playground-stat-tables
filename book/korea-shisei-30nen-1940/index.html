施政三十年史 1940

Shisei sanjūnen shi (Thirty Years of Administration [in Korea]), the Government-General of Korea's official history, 1910–1940
朝鮮總督府, 京城, 1940 (昭和十五年)

Only the tables and the chronology (1,230 events) are transcribed; the running text, the photographs, the organisation charts and the 目次 are not. The tables (125, about 6,000 figure cells) are printed in small kanji numerals, many of them sideways and squeezed, with 一/二/三 as stacks of bars. Each spread was first read by a Claude Sonnet agent; about 1,600 cells it could not read (whole tables in places) were then re-read by Claude Opus agents from tight, rotated and enlarged crops, which also corrected about 60 misread figures. 126 cells remain [?]; 12 that a printed total fixes exactly (one unknown, every other figure read) are filled in and shown with a blue background (inferred). Printed totals were re-added; those that do not add up are kept as printed and noted, and where a stack of bars could be grouped more than one way and exactly one grouping made a printed total reconcile, that grouping is used and noted.
