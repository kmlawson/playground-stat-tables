Annual Report of the Minister of State for Education 1905–06

Thirty-third Annual Report of the Minister of State for Education, for the Thirty-eighth Statistical Year of Meiji (1905–6), abridged
Department of Education (Mombushō), Tokyo, 1908

Each page was read once by a Claude Haiku 5.5 agent (the text sections) or a Claude Sonnet 5.5 agent (the denser tables), from full-resolution crops, with sideways pages turned upright; pages the first Haiku reader marked hard were read again from scratch by a Sonnet 5.5 agent, whose reading replaces the first. Nothing was checked by eye. Unreadable figures are marked [?]; totals that do not add up are kept as printed. Only tables are transcribed; the running text, the errata list, and the maps and diagrams at the end are not.
