About this book

観光地と洋式ホテル (Tourist Resorts and Western-Style Hotels), [昭和9年] · 鐵道省 (Ministry of Railways), 東京, [1934]

A Ministry of Railways guide to the Western-style hotels of Japan's tourist resorts and cities, and of Taiwan, Korea and Manchuria, in the mid-1930s: 65 hotels at 37 places from Tokyo, Yokohama, Hakone, Nikkō and Karuizawa to Unzen, Taihoku, Keijō, the Diamond Mountains, Dairen, Mukden and Shinkyō. Each entry gives the address, telephone, manager, number of rooms (single and double, with or without bath), room rates on the European or American plan, and meal prices; summer hotels give their season.

Every hotel entry is transcribed (65, matching the contents); the short descriptions of each place, the photographs, the notes on staying at a hotel and the contents are not. Each spread was read once by Claude Opus 5.5 from enlarged crops; figures are kept in kanji numerals as printed (、 is the decimal point, 〇 zero). Printed room totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted.
