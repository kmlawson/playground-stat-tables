家計調査報告 1939–40

Kakei chōsa hōkoku (Report of the Household Budget Survey), 自昭和十四年九月至昭和十五年八月 (September 1939 – August 1940)
內閣統計局 (Cabinet Bureau of Statistics), 東京; issued by 東京統計協會, 4 September 1941 (昭和16年9月4日)

Every table is transcribed: the small tables in the explanation of terms, the national and city tables of sections 二–四, the table of households allotted in the survey regulations, and the table of account-book keepers allotted and selected (七); the prose, the regulations and the account-book forms (六) are not. Each spread was read once by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from tight, contrast-raised crops of a dark scan (Arabic figures). The national tables run over several spreads, each spread carrying half the columns or a block of rows: the pieces are joined into one table per printed table, with amounts (實數) and percentages (比例) as separate parts, and a check that no cell was lost or doubled. Where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted; other doubtful digits are marked [?], and printed totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. The volume's errata slip (正誤表, 25 corrections) is attached as a note to each table it concerns; the figures stay as printed. 48 of about 30,100 filled cells could not be read and are marked [?].
