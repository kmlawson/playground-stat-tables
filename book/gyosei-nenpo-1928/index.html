日本帝國委任統治地域行政年報 1928

Nihon teikoku inin tōchi chiiki gyōsei nenpō (Annual Report on the Administration of the Territory under Japanese Mandate), 千九百二十八年度 (for 1928), published 昭和4年12月 (December 1929)
外務省 (Ministry of Foreign Affairs), 東京, 昭和四年

All tables in the report and its two appendices are transcribed; the prose, the organisation charts, the contents and the texts of the laws are not. The tables are printed vertically in kanji numerals (一五、四七四); they are written here horizontally in Arabic figures, with 、 kept as a comma (also where it serves as a decimal mark, as in the 方里 areas). Printed totals were re-added by the readers: totals that do not add up are kept as printed and noted. Where only the grouping of stacked 一/二/三 strokes was in doubt and exactly one grouping made a printed total reconcile, that grouping was used; every such cell was re-read by a reader who had not seen the first reading, and cells not confirmed are marked [?]. The trade tables, the 1927–28 budget and settled-accounts tables and the deaths-by-cause table were transcribed a second time independently; where the two readings differed a third blind reading decided, and cells on which no two readings agreed are marked [?].
