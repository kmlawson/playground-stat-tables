朝鮮總督府統計年報 1922 第5編

Chōsen Sōtokufu tōkei nenpō (Statistical Annual of the Government-General of Korea), 大正十一年 (Taishō 11), Part 5: 警察・衛生
朝鮮總督府, 京城, 1924 (大正十三年)

All 32 numbered tables are transcribed (32 tables, about 24,241 figure cells), with the contents; the 凡例, the contents pages and the colophon are not. Two frames are re-shot duplicates and were skipped. The scan of this part is soft and low in contrast, so more cells are unreadable than in the other parts. Tables printed across both pages of a spread are joined into one; long tables were read in sections by different readers and joined. Each spread was read once by a Claude Sonnet agent from tight crops, with no second reading. 893 cells could not be read and are marked [?]. Printed totals were re-added; those that do not add up are kept as printed and noted, and where a digit was ambiguous only between 3 and 8 and exactly one reading made a printed total reconcile, that reading is used and noted. Where the original prints a question mark (unknown figures for early years) it is kept as ?.
