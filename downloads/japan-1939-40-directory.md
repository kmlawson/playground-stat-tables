## List of Clubs, Societies, Associations, etc.

### Political and Military

**DAI ASIA KYOKAI** (The Great Asia Association) General Sekkon Matsui and others are directors. The society works for the unification of Asiatic countries. Issues "Dai Asia Shugi" (Great Asianism). Address: Osaka Building, Uchisaiwaicho, Kojimachi, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**GIYU ZAIDAN KAIBOGIKAI** (The Coastal Defence Volunteers' Association). Established in 1922; Otojiro Ito, president; membership 9,359. The object of the association is to study matters in connection with the coastal defence of Japan. Issues "Kaibo" (Coastal Defence). Address: Shisei Kaikan, Hibiya Park, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**KAIGUN KYOKAI** (The Navy League of Japan). Established in 1917; Chuichi Ariyoshi, M. H. P. president; membership 202,287. The Association is trying to spread a knowledge of the navy of Japan. Issues "Umi no Nippon" (Japan in the Seas). Address: Yusen Building, Marunouchi, Kojimachi, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**KAIKOSHA** (Military Club). Established in 1877; General Hisaichi Terauchi, president. The purpose is to keep friendly relations among army officers as well as to cultivate the spirit of patriotism. Address: Iidamachi, Kojimachi, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**KOKUMIN BOKU KYOKAI** (The National Aerial Defence Association). Kumataro Uyama, president. Address: 775, Sendagaya 4-chomé, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**OSAKA TOSHI KYOKAI** (The Association for the Study of Municipal Problems of Osaka). Established in 1925: Takéo Kagami, president; membership 3,000. The aim is to study every problem pertaining to cities. Address: Osaka City Hall, Nakanoshima, Kitaku, Osaka. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**SUIKOSHA** (The Naval Club). Established in 1876; Admiral Mitsumasa Yonai, president; membership 14,800. The purpose of the club is to study marine affairs and keep mutual friendship among members. Issues "Suikosha Kiji" (Naval Club Reports). Address: 13, Sakaécho, Shiba, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**TEIKOKU ZAIGO GUNJINKAI** (The Ex-service Men Association). Established in 1910; General Ikutaro Inouye, president; membership 3,000,000. The purpose is to keep up the spirit of the Japanese soldiery. Address: 5, Kudan 1-chomé, Kojimachi, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**TOKYO SHISEI CHOSAKAI** (The Tokyo Institute for Municipal Research). Baron Yoshiro Sakatani, president. Issues "Toshi Mondai" (City Problems). Address: 2, Hibiya Park, Kojimachi, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**TOSHI KENKYUKAI** (The Institute for the Study of Municipal Problems). Established in 1917; Nobumasa Suétsugu, president; membership about 5,000. Issues "Toshi Kōron" (City Review). Address: The Department of Home Affairs, Kojimachi, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**YOYOKAI** Established in 1923; Saburo Momotaké, director. Social friendship among naval officers is the aim of the society. Address: Care of Usagawa, 22, Kasumicho, Azabu, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**YUSHUKAI** Established in 1913: Admiral Isamu Takeshita, president. The purpose of the Society is to study various problems of the Japanese navy. Issues "Yushu". Address: Care of the Suikosha, Sakaécho, Shiba, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))

**ZENKOKU CHOSONCHO KAI** (The All-Japan Town and Village Masters' Society). Established in 1921; Tsutomu Okazaki, president; membership 11,044. The aim of the society is to study every problem concerning town and village administration. Issues "Jichi Kōron" (Autonomy Review). Address: 35 Nishikubo-Tomoécho, Shiba, Tokyo. — p. 1181 ([scan leaf 1237](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1939-1940/page/n1237/mode/1up))
