## Watch — Fire Brigade — Fire Record, 1882

**1882, January 21st** 11.30 a.m. Fire broke out in Tiendong Road near Honan Road. Five houses destroyed. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, February 27th** 10.30 a.m. Fire broke out at No. 45 Tiendong Road; but was quickly extinguished. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, March 28th** 12 noon. Fire broke out at Messrs. Russell & Co.'s Silk-reeling Factory on Soochow creek, but was soon extinguished. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, April 8th** 10 p.m. Fire broke out in an opium shop in Hoopeh Road. One house burnt and two pulled down. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, April 12th** 6.20 p.m. Fire broke out in North Honan Road. Three Chinese houses, the Japanese Hotel and six Foreign houses destroyed. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, April 20th** 9.20 a.m. Fire reported to have broken out in Messrs. Hall & Holtz store, but proved to be ignition of some spirits of wine from a lighted candle held by a coolie. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, April 28th** 9.30 a.m. Fire broke out in Messrs. Fearon Low & Co.'s premises, but was soon extinguished. Caused by contact of stove pipe with wood of roof at back of premises. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, August 4th** 12 noon. Fire broke out at Tannery on the Pootung side, burned fiercely and destroyed main and outbuildings,—a godown only saved. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, September 11th** 11 a.m. Fire broke out in village near the Horse Bazaar, but was soon extinguished. Three houses burnt, and one demolished. — p. 139 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, December 6th** 11 p.m. Fire broke out in North Kiangse Road, and five Chinese houses were destroyed. — p. 140 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, December 6th** 11.30 p.m. Fire broke out in the Nankin Road, No. 145P., but was soon extinguished, though the fire threatened to be of serious proportions at first. — p. 140 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, December 16th** 9.20 p.m. Fire broke out in the Pakhoi Road and resulted in four houses being burned, and one pulled down. — p. 140 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))

**1882, December 26th** 6.15 p.m. Fire broke out in the Upper Kwangse Road and four two-storeyed houses were totally burnt and one pulled down. — p. 140 ([scan leaf 143](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1882-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1883/page/n143/mode/1up))
