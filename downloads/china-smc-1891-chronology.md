## Watch — Fire Brigade — Fire Record, 1891

**1891 January 13th, 9.15 p.m.** Fire broke out at the corner of Foochow and Kwangse Roads. Caused by some straw coming in contact with a cooking-stove. Cook-house partially destroyed. Extinguished by Police and inmates. General alarm given. — p. 217 ([scan leaf 226](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1891-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1892/page/n226/mode/1up))

**1891 January 16th, 12.57 a.m.** Fire broke out at No. 8, Szechuen Road. Damage trifling. Cause unknown. Extinguished by some neighbours. General alarm given. — p. 217 ([scan leaf 226](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1891-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1892/page/n226/mode/1up))

**1891 January 23rd, 4 a.m.** Fire broke out in unoccupied house No. 148, Kweichow Road. Incendiarism suspected, as the house was saturated with kerosine. Damage trifling. Extinguished by Police. No general alarm given. — p. 217 ([scan leaf 226](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1891-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1892/page/n226/mode/1up))
