## Politics — [Progress of Parliamentary Movement in Japan]

**** 1. The Emperor's oath, on the occasion of his accession, that "public matters shall be decided by public assembly." — p. 35 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**1873** 2. In 1873 Itagaki and others petitioned the Government to take steps for realizing the Imperial Oath. — p. 35 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**1880** 3. In 1880 a national organization for promoting the cause of popular rights was effected and in its name a petition was submitted to the Emperor for the immediate establishment of a parliament. — p. 36 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**1880** 4. In 1880 Provincial Assembly Regulations were enacted and popular craving was partially satisfied. — p. 36 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**1881** 5. In 1881 Itagaki and his fellow-politicians organized the Liberal Party, and passed a resolution to push the movement with vigor. — p. 36 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**[1881]** 6. In the same year a proclamation was issued announcing the establishment of a national assembly ten years hence. — p. 36 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**1882** 7. In 1882 Okuma organized the Progressive Party. — p. 36 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**1884** 8. In 1884 the Civic Corporation Regulations were enacted. — p. 36 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**February 11th, 1889** 9. On February 11th, 1889, the Constitution was promulgated. — p. 36 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))

**[1890], October** 10. In October of the following year the first session of the Diet was convoked. — p. 36 ([scan leaf 64](https://archive.org/details/japan-year-book-1905/page/n64/mode/1up))
