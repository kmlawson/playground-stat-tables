## Watch — Fire Brigade — Fire Record, 1885

**1885, January 11th** 8.30 a.m. Fire broke out at the house, occupied by Captain Pike, No. Woosung Road. Caused through wood-work in the chimney. One room partly burnt. Extinguished by Fire Brigade and Police. General alarm given. — p. 203 ([scan leaf 206](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1885-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1886/page/n206/mode/1up))

**1885, January 16th** 1.45 a.m. Fire broke out at 312, Nankin Road. Caused by the ignition of some clothing from an opium-lamp. Put out by the Police. Damage trifling. No alarm given. — p. 203 ([scan leaf 206](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1885-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1886/page/n206/mode/1up))

**1885, January 25th** 2 p.m. Fire broke out at 33, Nankin Road. Caused through carelessness of a man smoking. Extinguished by the Police. Damage trifling. No alarm given. — p. 203 ([scan leaf 206](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1885-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1886/page/n206/mode/1up))

**1885, January 30th** 12.15 p.m. Fire broke out at No. 159, Chekiang Road. A kerosine-lamp exploded, igniting some cotton. Put out by the occupants. Damage very trifling. No alarm given. — p. 203 ([scan leaf 206](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1885-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1886/page/n206/mode/1up))

**1885, February 4th** 8.20 p.m. Fire broke out at No. 199, Chekiang Road. Caused through burning coals falling among some straw. Extinguished by the Police. Very little damage was done. No alarm given. — p. 203 ([scan leaf 206](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1885-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1886/page/n206/mode/1up))

**1885, February 10th** 8.30 p.m. Fire broke out at No. 28, in an alley off Nankin Road. A lighted candle coming in contact with a bed-curtain. Bed slightly burned. Extinguished by occupants. No alarm given. — p. 203 ([scan leaf 206](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1885-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1886/page/n206/mode/1up))

**1885, February 15th** 10.45 p.m. Fire broke out at 550, Foochow Road. Caused by a person who wilfully set fire to a table in an unoccupied house. Extinguished by the neighbours. Trifling damage. No alarm given. — p. 203 ([scan leaf 206](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1885-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1886/page/n206/mode/1up))

**1885, February 16th** 9 p.m. Fire broke out at No. 73, in an alley off Ningpo Road. Carelessness of a servant in the cookhouse. Extinguished by the Police and men of the Silk Guild Fire Extincteur Brigade. Cookhouse demolished. No alarm given. — p. 204 ([scan leaf 206](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1885-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1886/page/n206/mode/1up))
