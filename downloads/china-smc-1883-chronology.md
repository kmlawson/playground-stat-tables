## Watch — Fire Brigade — Fire Record, 1883

**1883, January 11th** 1 p.m. Fire broke out at 583, Chekiang Road. Four houses burnt down; four others pulled down. Extinguished by Fire Brigade and Police with extincteurs. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, February 1st** 10.45 p.m. Fire broke out in French Concession. Ten houses burnt down. Extinguished by Fire Brigade and Police. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, February 9th** 7.25 p.m. Fire broke out near City Creek bridge, French Bund. Four houses destroyed. Extinguished by Fire Brigade and Police. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, February 13th** 2 p.m. Kerosine oil lamp upset at 155, Old China Street, setting fire to planking. Was extinguished by neighbours. No alarm given. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, March 16th** 12.30 p.m. Fire broke out at house 1257, corner of Broadway and Nanzing Road. Fire put out by neighbours before any damage resulted. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, March 21st** 9.30 p.m. Fire broke out at 284, Taiwan Road. Two houses destroyed by fire and one pulled down. Brigade and Police attended. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, March 31st** 4 p.m. Fire broke out in an alley off the Chekiang Road. Four houses were destroyed. Fire Brigade and Police attended. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, June 3rd** 12.30 p.m. Fire reported in Foreign house, No. 36, Broadway. Sparks from cooking stove ignited shingles on roof. Extinguished by neighbours. No alarm given. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, June 6th** 6.25 a.m. Fire broke out in Japanese Shipping Office, North Yangtze Road. Police attended with extincteurs, but found the fire was put out by apparatus from S. S. "Hiroshima Maru." No alarm given. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, June 15th** 12.45 p.m. Fire broke out on the French Concession. Fire Brigade and Police attended. — p. 187 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, July 11th** Time not stated. Fire destroyed a small thatch house over a night-soil pit. Fired by some Portuguese, who were not arrested. No alarm was given. — p. 188 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, July 23rd** 10.38 p.m. Fire broke out in a cookhouse adjoining the Mitsu Bishi Mail Godowns, corner of Tsingpoo and North Yangtze Roads Cookhouse and a bamboo fence destroyed by fire. Fire Brigade and Police attended. — p. 188 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, July 26th** 3.40 a.m. Fire broke out at the junction of Canton and Honan Roads. A large number of houses were burnt. Supposed to be caused through Tea drying. Fire Brigade and Police attended. — p. 188 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, August 11th** 1.30 a.m. Fire broke out at 717, Seward Road, caused by sparks falling into a heap of shavings. Extinguished by inmates of house and two foreigners. No alarm given. — p. 188 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, September 10th** 5 a.m. Fire broke out at 717, Seward Road. Shavings in outer yard caught fire, which spread to roof of a shed. Extinguished by a Police constable and some of the inmates. No damage other than burning a hole in the roof of shed. No alarm given. — p. 188 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, November 2nd** 8.50 p.m. Fire broke out in the roof of No. 39, Broadway. Extinguished by owners servants before the arrival of the Police, with extincteurs. Caused by sparks from adjoining house. No alarm given. — p. 188 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, November 27th** 5 p.m. Fire broke out in the French Concession near the Honan Road Bridge. Three houses were destroyed. Extinguished by the hydrants of S. W. W. Co. Alarm given. — p. 188 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))

**1883, December 5th** 11.50 a.m. Fire broke out in the roof of No. 38, Broadway. Extinguished by neighbours before any damage was done. No alarm given. — p. 188 ([scan leaf 191](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1883-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1884/page/n191/mode/1up))
