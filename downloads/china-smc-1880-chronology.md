## Watch — Fire Brigade — Fire Record, 1880

**1880, January 10th** 10.15 p.m., in a store, 252-253, North Soochow Road. Extinguished by occupants. No general alarm given. Origin unknown. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, February 3rd** 4 a.m., at a Livery Stable in Hoopeh Road, opposite the Parsee Cemetery. 3 houses burnt and 1 pulled down. General alarm given. Brigade attended. Caused by a candle falling among some straw in the stable. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, February 5th** 3 a.m., at corner of Sunkiang and Kuangse Roads. General alarm given. Brigade attended. 2 one-storied houses burnt. Origin supposed to have been from a lamp left burning in a room having straw in it. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, March 30th** 12 noon, at 465, Canton Road. Bed burnt. No general alarm given. Caused by a candle setting fire to the bed curtains. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, April 1st** 5 p.m. A straw house in Lee Hongkew village. Burnt down. No general alarm given. Origin unknown. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, April 1st** 8.30 p.m. At a house being built in the Nankin Road, near the Bund (behind E. D. Sassoon & Co.'s). General alarm given. A quantity of worked up material more or less burned, building not injured. Originated probably from some workmen smoking, and setting fire to shavings. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, April 3rd** 4.35 a.m., in godown and coal shed China Merchants' Wharf, Hongkew. General alarm given. Godown and contents burnt. Origin unknown. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, April 21st** 12 midnight, at 48, Canton Road (in an alley near the Honan Road). General alarm given. 7 houses burnt. Origin unknown. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, May 12th** 7.35 p.m., at 586, Canton Road (in an alley near Rothwell, Love & Co.'s old premises). General alarm given. 1 house burnt, 2 partially burnt. Origin, fire in a cooking stove setting fire to the cook house. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, June 12th** 2.40 p.m. the cook house and boys' quarters at 17, Pekin Road (Hyde, Hertz & Co.'s). General alarm given. Cook house and boys' quarters burnt down. Caused by a rafter which was built in the cook house chimney catching fire. — p. 125 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, July 7th** 2.30 a.m., in a carpenter's shop in Lee Hongkew village. No general alarm given. 3 small houses burnt and 2 damaged. Put out by police and neighbours. Origin unknown. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, July 30th** 1 p.m. An ice house in country near Bamboo Town. Burnt down. No general alarm given. Origin unknown. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, August 22nd** 3.15 a.m., at 1099a, Broadway (corner of Minghong Road). General alarm given. 5 houses burnt. A Chinese boy aged 13, burnt. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, September 29th** 11 p.m., at 180, Hankow Road. A newly built empty house. Put out by neighbours. No General alarm given. Probably set on fire. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, October 5th** 4 a.m., at 69, Canton Road. A child dropped a match in a tin of kerosine oil which ignited and ran out on the floor. Put out by a foreign constable and others. No general alarm given. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, October 6th** 3 a.m. At a paper shop in Fuhkien Road, near the Chinese theatre. General alarm given. 66 houses burnt and 7 houses pulled down. Origin, explosion of a kerosine lamp. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, October 27th** 12 midnight, 254, Woosieh Road. No general alarm given. Woodwork front of house partially burnt. Origin unknown. Empty house, supposed to have been set on fire wilfully. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, November 14th** 3.25 a.m., at 135, Woochang Road (a Bakery). General alarm given. The contents of a room over the oven burnt. Origin, a defective chimney. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, November 16th** 11 p.m., a stack of reeds at back of 487A, Broadway Road. General alarm given. No damage to any buildings. Origin unknown. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, November 26th** 4.30 a.m., a thatched house in the Lee Hongkew village. No general alarm given. Roof burnt, put out by Police and neighbours. Origin unknown, probably wilfully set on fire. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, November 26th** 10.30 p.m., 400M, (near H.B.M.'s Gaol). No general alarm given. Woodwork of the back of the house partially burnt. Empty house, door open. Origin of fire unknown, probably set on fire. — p. 126 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, November 28th** 5 a.m., at 354, Lee Hongkew village. No general alarm given. Bed and bedding burnt, put out by inmates. Origin, upsetting a lamp which set the bed on fire. — p. 127 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, November 29th** 11.30 p.m., at a house in Chefoo Road. No general alarm given, part of ceiling burnt, caused by a stove pipe setting fire to some woodwork near it. — p. 127 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, December 2nd** 4 p.m., at 121, Hankow Road. General alarm given. The Verandah and part of the roof of house burnt, put out by inmates and neighbours. Origin, a chimney built under the floor of the verandah. — p. 127 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, December 8th** 10.45 p.m., at a house in Amoy Road, near H.B.M.'s Gaol. General alarm given. Roof of cook house and part of house burnt, put out with extincteurs. Origin unknown. — p. 127 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, December 21st** 11.45 p.m., at 700, Pekin Road. No general alarm was given. Front of house (woodwork) slightly burned. Origin, supposed wilfully set on fire. — p. 127 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))

**1880, December 24th** 12.10 a.m., a large buffaloe shed off the Broadway Road (in the country). General alarm given. The sheds and 3 buffaloes burnt. Origin of fire unknown. — p. 127 ([scan leaf 129](https://archive.org/details/Shanghai-Municipal-Council-Report-for-the-Year-1880-and-Budget-for-the-Year-1881/page/n129/mode/1up))
