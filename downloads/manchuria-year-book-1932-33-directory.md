## Social Welfare Institutions

### General Social Institutions.—

**Manchuria Social Work Research Society** (Dairen) Established 1922 Conducts research in social welfare, social problems and social policy; operates a personal advisory office; publishes a monthly magazine called "The Society of Manchuria." — p. 491 ([scan leaf 586](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n586/mode/1up))

**Hekizanso (碧山莊)** (Dairen) Established 1911 Provides lodgings and shops of daily necessities for Chinese coolies engaged in working cargo at Dairen wharfs; gives a grant towards compensation for damages caused by the fault of coolies, and also a grant to the mutual relief fund; gives free medical treatment and condolence-money to the family in case of the death of coolies; also has a recreation department, and aims at general welfare of the coolies. At present it is under the management of the Fukusho Kakokai, and is always able to house about 10,000 coolies. — p. 491 ([scan leaf 586](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n586/mode/1up))

**Cart-coolies Lodging House** (Dairen) Established 1911 Established under a partnership organization to lodge rikisha men and cart drivers of Dairen; is equipped with bachelors' quarters, horse fodder storehouse, rikisha shed, restaurant and bath room. — p. 491 ([scan leaf 586](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n586/mode/1up))

**Cart Shed** (Dairen) Established 1919 Established by officials of the Dairen Chamber of Commerce and others. In the adjoining Shufang (書房) or library, children of the poor are given education. — p. 491 ([scan leaf 586](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n586/mode/1up))

### Charity and Relief Institutions.—

**Imperial Charity Fund** (Port Arthur) Established 1920 Makes grants to charity and relief enterprises in the Kwantung Leased Territory, amounting to ¥10,000 a year, and fosters the development of social welfare works. — p. 491 ([scan leaf 586](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n586/mode/1up))

**Port Arthur Branch of Kamakura Orphanage** (Port Arthur) Established 1913 Relief and protection of orphans and poor children, and reformation of depraved children. — p. 491 ([scan leaf 586](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n586/mode/1up))

**Salvation Army Home for Children and Women** (Dairen) Established 1906 Housing and protection of orphans, poor children and unfortunate women. — p. 491 ([scan leaf 586](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n586/mode/1up))

**Labour Protection Society** (Dairen) Established 1906. Protection and housing of released Japanese prisoners, the unemployed and the poor. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))

**Ijinkai Foundation** (Dairen) Established 1921. Protection of released prisoners. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))

**Manchuria Dojinkai** (Dairen) Established 1927. Relief of the unemployed, advice on personal matters, and employment agency. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))

**Chiko-in Lodging House** (Dairen) Established 1925. Free lodging for the destitute. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))

**Dairen Municipal Social Welfare Hall** (Dairen) Established 1921. Protection of the unemployed, lodging house, free preparation and writing of documents and letters, advice on personal matters, municipal pawnshop. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))

**Dairen Municipal Dwelling Houses** (Dairen) Established 1922. Renting of small dwelling houses. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))

### Free Medical Institutions.

**Manchurian Committee of the Japan Red Cross Society** (Headquarters in the Kwantung Government Office) Established 1906. Total Membership 83,112 (42,180 Japanese and 40,932 Chinese). Besides the headquarters, there are 15 branches and 17 sub-branches in North and South Manchuria. The present principal activities are the training of nurses, distribution of relief parties, relief work (Mukden Hospital and 13 relief stations), prevention and treatment of tuberculosis, and protection of children. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))

**Dairen Charity Hospital** (Dairen) Established 1906. Gives free medical treatment to poor Japanese and Chinese, and to others at cost charges; takes care of the sick with the commission of the Civil Administration Office and the Dairen Municipal Office. In 1919 a training school for nurses was opened. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))

**Fushun Koji-in** (Fushun) Established by missionaries of the Higashi Honganji Temple, gives free medical treatment and free medicine to the Chinese poor. — p. 492 ([scan leaf 587](https://archive.org/details/manchuria-year-book-1932-33/page/n587/mode/1up))
