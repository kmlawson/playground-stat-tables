The strongest subject additions are **writing-systems-and-phonetics** (three alphabet/sound-symbol tables) and **anthropometry** (two cephalic-index distributions). **Regional-products** adds a useful qualitative product-geography concept across agriculture, mining and manufacture.

**Route-distances** has eighteen reference matrices, mostly with existing shipping or inland-navigation fits. It would improve precise retrieval across modes. **Asbestos** is similarly an optional commodity refinement: all three output tables already fit industrial-output, and they repeat one company's series. Neither proposal justifies withholding an existing valid tag.

**Public-holidays-and-commemorations** should be decided against a deliberate scope extension of calendars-and-time. **Diplomatic-claims** should be decided against broadening indemnities to include pending compensation demands. **Foreign-trade-relations** is lower priority: its two examples repeat the same historical list of first trading contacts.

## Existing vocabulary covers most apparent gaps

- The 28 press directories need existing newspapers and/or periodicals tags, rather than a new combined term. Daily frequency is not the defining distinction, but the publication titles and context establish their kinds. Some continuations contain only newspapers or only periodicals.
- Shipping explicitly includes sea routes; inland-navigation includes river voyage routes. Railway accounts, including stores and invested capital, are explicitly within railways.
- Postal workforce tables fit employment-and-unemployment, and substantial nationality breakdowns can use nationality-and-ethnicity without changing the primary communications topic.
- Vegetable-oils explicitly includes solidified vegetable oils, so vegetable tallow already fits. Horticulture includes orchards, covering walnut production.
- Public-property covers the Shantung valuations; taxation covers octroi; companies covers incorporation and manufacturer counts; imperial-household explicitly includes succession.
- Army and honours-and-ranks cover the military glossaries; army and police can coexist in a mixed gendarmerie table. No glossary-format or paramilitary catch-all is necessary.
- The fiscal/foreign-trade/shipping overview fits economic-indicators, whose definition includes broad summaries, not only index-number sets.

## Deferred records

- `china-1925-6:p1022_1`: The body provides unlabeled half-yearly index values and the context does not name the quantity. The raw heading mentions copper coins/banknotes, but it does not establish whether the measure is an exchange rate, price index or another monetary indicator. A new generic index tag would hide the missing subject; leave for source-context clarification without OCR or numeric repair.
- `china-1926-7:p0153_1`: The table says only Yu Seng Co. output, with years and quantities but no product or units. Its section spans molybdenum, bismuth, arsenic and petroleum and does not establish extraction versus manufacture. Do not invent a material-specific tag or a new unknown-output subject; the missing identity needs source-context clarification.
